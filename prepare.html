// The_Arena

Two banks. Built by you.
Attacked by you.

JengaSec builds two live copies of the same bank, JengaBank, out of software written by student teams. Each team owns one component. When the build is finished, other teams attack it, and an AI defence agent watches the whole enterprise and decides what to do about it. You do not build a bank. You build one service inside one, against a published interface contract, with a fixed list of security requirements and a fixed list of events it must emit. Four people can finish it.

ENTERPRISE_A
  • 7 to 9 application cells
  • 2 to 3 AI defence cells
ENTERPRISE_B
  • 7 to 9 application cells
  • 2 to 3 AI defence cells

Every brief is built twice, by two different teams, in two separate enterprises. The comparison between them is part of what is judged.

Full application briefs, with every security requirement a judge will check, are issued in the welcome pack when registration completes.

// Eligibility

Teams of three to four.
Ages 18 to 25.

✓

Age 18 to 25 at the date of registration, verified from an identity document.

✓

Open to young builders, not only registered students. If you are studying, register with your institutional email: where places are close, verified institutions are preferred.

✓

Teams of three to four people, plus one optional reserve who may substitute once with organiser approval. Four is the intended size and every brief is scoped for it.

✓

One person, one team, one side. Nobody may be on both a Blue and a Red team.

✓

Teams may mix institutions, or have none. The captain's institution is recorded as the lead institution.

✓

Mentors may advise. They may not write any part of a submission, and their involvement must be declared on the cover page.

// How_Selection_Works

Proposals are competitive.
That is by design.

UP TO 0 PROPOSALS PER BRIEF
→
TOP 0 ADVANCE TO BUILD
→
+0 HELD AS RESERVE

4 to 6 AI Defence teams advance in total across both enterprises.

AI Red runs about three attacking teams for every defending team.

Application Red teams register and are assigned to a cell: they skip the proposal round.

Every team that submits receives a written feedback report: criterion scores, the judges' comments and the evidence cited, whether or not it is selected. Appeals within 72 hours of results, on procedural grounds only.

Places are limited and awarded competitively. Submitting a proposal is not a place, and there is no waitlist promotion after selection closes. The reserve team named at selection is the only fallback.

// What_You_Submit

Twelve sections.
A missing one scores zero.

A missing required section scores zero on its criterion. It is not averaged away, discounted, or inferred from elsewhere in the document. Start drafting these now: your proposal can be written before the platform exists.

HEADINGS ARE NOT OPTIONAL :: Use the section numbers and titles below as your document headings, in order, exactly as written, for example 08 Threat Model. The platform reads headings to locate each section before a judge opens the file. A section under a different title, or merged into another, can be read as missing and scored as missing.

// The_Clock

The deadline is fixed.
Only its consequences are calibrated.

All deadlines published in the platform in East Africa Time (UTC+3), enforced by the platform clock, not yours.
The window closes automatically. No organiser can accept a late file. No individual extensions for any team, for any reason.
Reminders at 7 days, 2 days, and 12 hours are a courtesy. Their absence is not a defence.
Upload as many versions as you wish. Only the latest is judged.
Platform faults: email within 30 minutes from the captain's registered address, with the file and the error.

Upload a complete draft at least twenty-four hours early and improve it afterwards. An uploaded draft is a submission. An unuploaded perfect document is not.

Late Submission
Not accepted, no score.
Missing Declarations
Returned incomplete, not judged if uncorrected.
Integrity Breaches
Plagiarism, fabricated citations or evidence, outside authorship. Zero and referral or disqualification.
